Cozy Space SFX — 46 retro sound effects

Short chiptune-style sound effects for sci-fi and arcade games: weapons, explosions,
pickups, movement, damage, UI, station machinery and short jingles.

Format: WAV, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit, mono, normalized to a similar loudness (peaks under -1 dBFS).
Every file starts and ends in silence, so they can be triggered back to back.
